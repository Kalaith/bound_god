## After the session (ask, do not lead)

- Who was your disciple? What happened to them, and why?
- Did you care what happened to them? (Their words; then 1 not at all … 5 very much.)
- Was the Favour you spent on them worth it? What did it cost you elsewhere?
- What had changed when you came back from meditating? What surprised you?
- Was there a moment you did not know what to do next? What did the game tell you then?
