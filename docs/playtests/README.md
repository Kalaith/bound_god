# Fresh player sessions

Use these notes to record how a new player experiences the game. Two kits:

- `TEMPLATE.md`: the village slice's first playable moment (GAME_DESIGN.md §17.1). The M13 target is for at least two of three fresh players to reach it in under an hour without help.
- `TEMPLATE_CULTIVATION.md`: the cultivation slice and its key test (GAME_DESIGN.md §17.2), from M18. Targets: a sect forms and the player witnesses a first tribulation within two hours (plan §1.1), and at least two of three players say they cared what happened to their disciple. The slice is long: run it as one sitting of up to two hours, and let the player meditate when they choose; if they never meditate by the end, note it and ask them to try it for the last ten minutes.

The game now speaks to a new player through prompts in the top-left panel (`H` puts one away for that world). Record which prompts appeared, whether the player read them, and which they put away unread; do not reset them between steps.

## Run a session

1. Start a new world for someone who has not seen the game. Start the timer when the valley first appears.
2. Ask them to play as they normally would. Do not explain controls, point things out, suggest actions, or answer how-to questions. Record questions instead.
3. Stay quiet and take notes with elapsed times. Keep the timer running during stalls and menus. Stop it when they reach the moment or end the session.
4. Copy the right template for each player and fill it in during or just after the session.

Record the build and version, hardware, total time, which steps they reached and when, stalls and their duration, what the player said, and any free observations. Use elapsed `mm:ss` times from when the valley appears. Mark a step not reached if the session ends first.

Give each player a short code (P1, P2, …) and never write real names. Save one completed record per player in this folder as `YYYY-MM-DD-<tester-code>.md`. Keep the notes here so later sessions can be compared with what changed.
