## Decisions

- Goals, steps, plans, targets and messages are plain Dictionaries (not RefCounted classes) so agent state saves with `var_to_bytes` without per-class conversion. Goal identity is `kind << 32 | target`.
- Dirt-family blocks (grass, dirt, sand, gravel) yield and cost **soil**, a divine-stock-only resource; settlements never stock it. Keeps "placing consumes one unit" true without making terraforming free.
- A log yields 4 wood (a log makes four planks); tall grass yields 2 thatch. Other blocks yield 1.
- Doors are passable one-block panels; a doorway is two stacked door blocks so a 2-cell-tall person fits. Room probes treat doors as walls.
- `sim_checks` also loads every script under `res://scripts` so presentation parse errors fail headlessly.

- The voxel grid is one flat PackedByteArray (index x + z*W + y*W*D) instead of a dictionary of chunk arrays: far faster in GDScript. Chunks stay the unit of meshing, dirtiness and saving.
- Plans only use structures the person believes in (`beliefs.structures`, filled by perception); a vanished one fails on arrival and is remembered as failed.
- Agents change the shared world only through `Effects` actions (take/deposit stock, forage, harvest, claim a bed, place blocks); the step reads the result from `agent.step_result` on a later tick, so conflicts resolve deterministically in id order.
- Player-facing text is split into `data/text.json` (UI, blocks, templates) and `data/text_agents.json` (goals, plans, fail reasons, activities, log, chronicle); labels share one namespace and duplicates fail validation.
- Thought texts and trait names stay in their own catalogs (`society.json`, `people.json`) beside their definitions; UI chrome lives in `data/text_ui.json`.
- Offered designs reach the residents of the shrine's settlement (in M8: those who know the god); seeing a finished building whose design was offered also teaches it.
- People keep what they carry through every change of mind; a plan that needs free hands first puts the load in the stores (`PlanHelpers.free_hands`), and Haul includes storing a carried load. Final Landing drops loads on every change of goal, which wasted most loads on the valley's long walks. Using the stores updates the user's belief about them.
- Navigation steps are at most one block up or down so every move can be walked back; harvesting never takes a block someone stands on, and anyone whose floor vanishes settles onto the nearest standable cell.
- World generation adds 154 berry bushes (rings near the tree and the hamlet), daily regrowth (none in winter), four stone outcrops near the tree and quarry faces on the ridge, because two travellers otherwise starved or could not find stone.
- Walking pace is Final Landing's 1 cell per tick; ground sleep restores 170/day; recreation decays 22/day.
- Check scenarios are untyped functions: a runtime error then returns null and fails, where `-> String` returned "" and passed silently.
- Builders never stand on the building they are raising; stand cells must be reachable by path.
- Runtime errors inside the simulation do not fail `sim_checks` (only errors in a check function do), so every check run is also grepped for `SCRIPT ERROR`.
- Offered designs reach residents of the shrine's settlement who know the god (seeing the avatar is enough).
- A lash-out break spends itself on one fight, then the break is sulking: repeated insults otherwise killed both people.
- RestoreShrine (confirmed by the user) is open to Believers (utility 0.35 × devotion) as well as the Devout (0.6 ×): with only the Devout the ridge altar was never restored by its own people.
- `tools/scratch/` holds local diagnostic probes and is git-ignored.
- Abstract model (M13 spike, leaning): records are the frozen agents themselves rather than a separate summary type, since agents are already plain saved data; the abstract model uses `sim.rng` in settlement id order and leaves per-settlement sub-streams to M22. Reason: least new state and code, identical rules; regions are the first place order independence matters.
- Who pursues a goal is read from `Sim.pursuers(goal_key)`, an index rebuilt when `goal_version` changes (every intention change bumps it, and every tick does). Code that sets `agent.intention` directly must bump `sim.goal_version`.
- People who find no route to a chest remember its stand (`cell:<stand>` in their failure memory) and try another chest for `agents.failure_memory_ticks`.
- `check_sleepers_sleep_the_night_through` accepts a sleeper woken by a bodily need (eating at 05:00 after going to bed hungry); it still fails anyone up for anything else before 05:00, which is what it was written to catch.
- Someone asleep at night keeps sleeping until the sleep hours end (`deliberation.gd` keeps the night sleep utility while asleep; the step no longer wakes a rested sleeper early). Before, rest reaching ~92 dropped sleep's utility and people got up to chat around 03:00 (6.8 h of broken sleep; now they wake at dawn).
- Faith pacing (user found it slow): a witnessed miracle gives 14 devotion (was 10), first sight 6 (was 4), so two or three miracles make a Believer within the starting Favour. The first-steps line shows the nearest person's devotion against the Believer threshold, or says when Favour is too low for any miracle.
- The crosshair shows "F · Inspect <name>" over a person; from the overlook the pick margin is a fixed 200 px instead of shrinking with distance.
- People learn what the chests hold when they use one, pass within reach of one, or at the day's end; they do not see into chests from afar.
- Key bindings saved before an action existed still load: the new action takes its default key, or the first free one.
- Valley layout: sacred tree at the valley centre (80,80), ridge ruin at (128,128) (~68 blocks), hamlet at (112,134). The tree's first circle (20 blocks) always holds a few trees, stone outcrops and berry bushes. The river runs beside the tree-ridge axis so nobody must cross water.

- A settlement's area belongs to the nearest centre where two overlap (`settlements.at_cell`), so a founded settlement keeps its ground when Tree Hollow grows toward it.
- Village criteria (decided for M13, tune from play): 10 people, all housed, a day of food in the chests, 4 homes, a storehouse, a hall, a garden and a living shrine. The hall needs no hearth so it can be built without stone.
- Gardens bear in winter (the wild bushes do not) so a village can be self-sustaining before M14's fields.
- The sacred tree is always active (user, 2026-10-10): reach 10 blocks without support, widening toward the full radius as support nears the threshold; no grace timer; 30 starting Favour.
- Travellers start knowing no designs; migrants bring them (user).
- The first-steps guide is optional ("Show first-steps guide" in settings, on by default) (user).
- Cultivation (M15, leaning or tuning): Mortal → Qi Refining needs no breakthrough (the threshold is crossed in meditation, chronicled); M16's breakthrough and tribulation begin at Qi Refining's peak. The tree teaches its technique once, to the first who reaches Qi Refining in dense qi while nobody living knows it; after that it passes only by teaching. Teaching uses the help-request message with a new need (teaching), and a teacher is willing unless they dislike the asker (opinion + 20 + kindness × 20 > 0), since the Way is shared more freely than bread. One sect per settlement; a sect is founded only where the qi at the settlement's centre is at least 0.5. Affinities in the slice are wood (faster in the tree's qi) and earth (a sounder foundation) besides none, because only effects that exist may be named. Sect names, doctrine labels and realm names live in `text_cultivation.json`; chronicle entries keep the sect's name as label keys (`sect_name|adj|noun`) and are worded on display. Defy Heaven lets the defied cultivator reach the new realm (the GDD's "imperfect breakthrough") rather than only survive; the check "no miracle grants a realm" means no miracle changes a realm by itself or before the person's own trial resolves. The storm follows the person if they move. Injured cultivators may try again once their progress climbs back. Sanctuary bonus raised from 0.15 to 0.2 after the first exit run (60% against 55% was too close to read; now 68% against 48%). Loners face Heaven beyond the sanctuary; that is the slice's only reason to go elsewhere. Witnesses pray from where they are (the prayer plan takes them toward the shrine, which reads as gathering); there is no separate gathering goal yet.
- The tree (M17, leaning or tuning): the tree learns only from trials in its sanctuary (the GDD says the tree learns from tribulations it witnesses beneath its branches). One trial is enough to unlock fruit, so the exit's "within a season" is easy; repeated lightning still deepens insight toward later unlocks (none exist yet, by the closed-catalog rule). Maturity's day rate (0.05) means the first phase change comes from insight, not years; in meditation (M18) the days will carry it. Scars are a record drawn on the bark (presentation), not block changes, so the sacred tree's blocks are never altered. The fruit decision is made by the master in deliberation (a ShareFruit goal), so it is a person's choice with a reason, never a system's.
- Meditation (M18, leaning or tuning): the abstract step grows with the span (1 h within a year, 2 h to ten years, 3 h beyond) so 50 years fit in the key test's minute; the 20-day consistency check runs at one hour. Abstract meditation takes three sittings a day, 0.15 + 0.35 × drive of each waking hour (× realm), tuned so the 20-day comparison matches the detailed world's cultivation (35 against 36). Prayers bring half the Favour while meditating (shrines.meditation_favour 0.5) so skipping years is not free. A pinned person's danger wakes the god once as it begins. Protect rules exist only for pinned people and only act while meditating (in play the god acts by hand). The key test's sect master is set old (115) so a succession can happen in its 50 years: an old cultivator otherwise keeps meditating toward the next realm, whose lifespan is far longer; this is the slice's honest behaviour, not a bug. Abstract building plans every third day and finds sites from 16 candidates, which may place buildings slightly differently from detailed founders. Raids and wolves pause while abstract.
- Sect architecture (M19, leaning or tuning): the hall's qi rule reads the tree's place from the map's middle (function rules see only blocks); a design on its own always meets it and the site finder decides where it may stand. The ground's distance from dwellings is a site rule, not part of the function, so an existing ground does not stop working when a house goes up beside it. Only a sect's master and elders propose its buildings, and they may do so even when their nature would not make them propose anything else (the plan's "a sect's leaders propose"). Hall factor 1.3; four members for a hall. Blueprints are kept in the god's state, last a season (10 days), and are taken by the first founder who wants the function and knows the design; nothing else changes (whispering Build still only raises Build). A settlement devises its own home only after 12 days without one, so the god's first offered design keeps its moment (on day 1 Tree Hollow knows none and devised a home at once). Devised designs are named in English when devised (template names are data). Paths keep while the god meditates; their navigation weight is 0.85 and the A* heuristic is unchanged (routes are near-shortest, not exact). Seekers bringing the sect designs (in the plan) is left out: it only shifted the random stream, and that stream led the abstract valley into a winter famine that ended the key test's sect (see Known issues). The M16 exit is now judged on the odds each trial was decided at (recorded in the trial) over 24 seeds, since M19's paths reshuffled the rolls and the 12-seed outcome rates flipped (57% against 58%) while the odds kept their gap.

An infirmary is also a dwelling (beds and a door), so people may live in it; recovering there works whoever lives there.
